Problem Statement

  • Atrial ectopy burden is among the strongest selectors for prolonged monitoring after stroke, but every threshold was derived on Holter ECG, the resource the selection rations; newer scores still take PACs from a single Holter, while scores that need no monitoring rank patients inconsistently (Gladstone et al., 2015; Haeusler et al., 2025; Maille et al., 2026; Helbitz et al., 2025).
  • Smartwatch beat intervals carry ectopy, yet performance is reported only as window classification; the error of a daily ectopic-beat count, and the loss when morphology is removed and flagged beats are discarded, have not been quantified (Mittal et al., 2021; Han et al., 2026).
  • Observation is informative, not random: analysable PPG is concentrated at night, while PACs peak in the afternoon and rise with activity; models usually delete missing data rather than model it, so a naive estimate under-counts ectopy, most in high-burden patients (Merschel & Reinhardt, 2022; Larsen et al., 2016; Johnson et al., 2021; Nijman et al., 2022).

Consequently, a smartwatch-derived ectopy value cannot currently be compared with ECG-derived thresholds or used to select post-stroke survivors for prolonged monitoring.

As such, this study attempts to quantify the information lost from PPG waveform to filtered beat intervals, develop a two-scale ST-GNN that estimates the ectopy rate using quality flags and unobserved time as input, and evaluate its deployability and selection value in post-stroke survivors.

About 1.5 minutes. Three linked problems: the measure is Holter-bound; wearable evidence stops at window classification; and the missing time is not random. The third point is new and comes from combining two literatures: Merschel and Reinhardt found analysable smartwatch PPG above 94% between midnight and 6 AM, and Larsen found PACs in frequent-PAC subjects lowest around 6 AM and peaking around 3 PM. So the clean hours are the low-PAC hours. Scaling a rate from analysable time to 24 hours will under-count. That is the core reason RO2 treats the quality flag and unobserved time as inputs.
