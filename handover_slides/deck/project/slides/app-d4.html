Patient sets P0 to P3 are disjoint (Table B.3). Fallback (§3.9): without the cohort by semester 4, coverage is modelled from Pulsewatch wear and RO4b is deferred.

Held for questions. No single dataset has everything. Icentia11k has the answer key, which beat is a PAC, but no wrist signal and no clock. Pulsewatch links the wrist to the heart: it shows how often a real heartbeat reaches the watch at each motion level, but it is a Samsung watch and its labels merge PACs with PVCs. The Garmin specification fixes what a flag and a gap mean. The cohort has the right watch and the right patients but no ECG, so it shows delivery, not accuracy. The order follows: RO1 decides the design, RO2 is frozen at M4, only then is the held-out set P3 opened for RO4a, and RO3 runs alongside so the core does not wait for ethics or the device.
