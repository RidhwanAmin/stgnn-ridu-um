Preliminary Findings (1): Cross-Device Pilot

A beat-graph ST-GNN on the same representation was built for rhythm classification from wearable PPG and tested on 33 held-out subjects from four cohorts with subject-level splits and re-curated external labels: no subject is shared between training, validation and test.

Evaluation mode. AUROCs at score weight 0.5 (untuned), 95% CIs from 2,000 subject resamples; recall at validation thresholds 0.393 / 0.505 (gfeat-head score), not re-tuned.

  • Ranking transferred; the operating point did not: AUROC ≥ 0.995 wherever non-AF windows existed, but AF recall at 0.393 fell to 45% on MIMIC-III.
  • PAC/PVC windows raised false AF alarms on Pulsewatch (18.3% vs 8.9% for sinus rhythm). One AF subject per cohort except the AF-only holdouts, where three subjects give 99% of windows. The design was iterated while viewing these holdouts, so they are not untouched.
  • What this pilot does not show: labels are per-window rhythm classes, not beat counts, and the task is AF rather than ectopy burden. It establishes feasibility of the representation, which RO1 and RO2 extend to counting.
