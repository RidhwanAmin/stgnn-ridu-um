Held for questions. Say the outcome first: the check did not pass under the rule locked before the run. Two criteria were met: beat matching stays well above its chance control even at high motion, and it falls with motion in every subject. The third failed: two peak detectors gave different absolute retention, 0.850 against 0.752, so the level depends on the detector. This test cannot say which detector is right. So no retention value is claimed; the motion term stays and is estimated in RO2 with a detector sensitivity analysis. The 8 subjects were chosen for the check, so this is not an estimate of the curve. The current lag method reached only 2.5% of excluded and 0.8% of no-label segments, so a label-free lag method is an RO2 task.
