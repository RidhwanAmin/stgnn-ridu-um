Icentia11k beat labels come from automatic detection reviewed by technologists and approved by a senior technologist (Tan et al., 2019); about 24% of beats are unclassifiable and are excluded. Segments are random ~70-min samples with no clock time, so the reference is a patient-level rate.

About 1.5 minutes. This slide answers the panel’s most likely question before it is asked: for every objective, what is the truth and what can we not claim. ECG truth exists for RO1, RO2 and RO4a. Icentia11k segments carry no clock time, so its reference is a patient-level rate, not a true day; RO4a uses patients held out from RO1 and RO2. RO3 and RO4b have no truth by design, so they are framed as feasibility and agreement. Be open about Icentia11k: labels are technologist-reviewed, not cardiologist-adjudicated, and a quarter of beats are unclassifiable. Pulsewatch includes a reference patch ECG, with three-expert 30-second labels.
