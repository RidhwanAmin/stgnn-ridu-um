Research Methodology: Overview

Phase 1 · RO1

Investigate information loss

Data: Pulsewatch (PPG, window labels); Icentia11k (ECG beat labels)

Analyses: waveform vs interval model; filter threshold sweep; premature vs PAC-only count

Outputs: morphology loss; share of ectopic beats filtered; count bias by burden

Phase 2 · RO2

Develop two-scale ST-GNN

Model: beat-graph per window; epoch-graph over the day; flag as input; frozen retention correction; coverage gate

Compared with: classical, rule-based and sequence baselines; ablations (remove a part, retest)

Outputs: daily-rate error; bias and limits of agreement

Phase 3 · RO3

Evaluate deployability

Cohort: ischaemic stroke or TIA; 7-day continuous wear; recruit about 40, analyse ≥34

Measures: yield; computability; day-to-day stability; activity; ESVEA proportion

Outputs: feasibility against progression criteria; abstention rate (days left unreported)

Phase 4 · RO4

Substitute and validate

4a: held-out (unseen during development) Icentia11k patients degraded to smartwatch-like intervals; validated against ECG labels

4b: cohort; wearable vs score-based selections

Outputs: reclassification rate; agreement and composition

Design: three retrospective secondary-data analyses (RO1, RO2, RO4a) and one prospective observational cohort (RO3, RO4b).
