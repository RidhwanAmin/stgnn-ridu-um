Appendix C3: Operational Definitions

TermDefinition used throughout this study
Beat-interval seriesThe sequence of pulse-to-pulse intervals a watch reports, without the waveform
Per-beat quality flagThe device confidence that a reported interval is a genuine beat
CoverageThe proportion of a monitoring period with analysable intervals, reported for each 6-hour block and for daytime and night-time
Valid dayA day meeting the pre-specified minimum coverage in both blocks
Ectopy rateRetention-weighted ectopic beats per analysable hour in each 6-hour block, combined by block length into a daily count
ESVEAPrimary: ≥480 supraventricular beats per day (Haeusler et al., 2025); secondary: ≥30 per hour (Binici et al., 2010); runs reported separately
Pulse deficitA premature beat that produces no detectable wrist pulse, so the watch reports one long interval instead of two
