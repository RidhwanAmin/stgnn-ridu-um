Held for questions, for example: what happens before the first patient wears a watch? Two tracks run in parallel until the start of S4. The readiness track starts with the device itself: we do not have it yet, so R1 is obtaining the watches, Garmin Health SDK access, and Garmin’s 24-hour logging confirmation in writing, since the proposal currently cites a personal communication. R2 is the device verification §3.5 requires before ethics: export, flag and gap encoding, 24-hour logging, 7-day battery, and one item added here, the accelerometer scale, because π̂(m) is fitted on Samsung motion and has to be mapped to Garmin units. Verification ends at M1 and fixes the thresholds the protocol states. R3 builds the Garmin pipeline on that verification data, so it is proven before any participant. R4 is the protocol, consent, case report form and the MREC submission after M1, ending at M2 with registration. R5 is site readiness at PPUM. The tracks meet three times: shared coverage code, a dry run of the estimator on real Garmin output, and the motion scale. At the start of S4: go, and recruitment starts; no-go, and §3.9 applies: RO3 becomes device verification with coverage modelled from Pulsewatch, RO4a is unchanged and RO4b is deferred. RO1, RO2 and RO4a never wait for the cohort.
