About 1 minute. This slide connects the preliminary work to the study. Each row is something already done, what it fixed in the design, and where it is tested next. The filtering result is why flagged beats stay as input, but it formed H1, so H1 is tested on patients not used before. The pilot shows interval graphs carry rhythm information, and that a threshold set on one device does not hold on another, which is why probabilities are calibrated and substitution is tested under device losses rather than assumed. Say the pre-check plainly: it did not pass its locked rule; the motion effect is real, but its level depended on the peak detector, so no retention value is claimed and RO2 estimates it with a detector sensitivity analysis. The clock-time check is why training uses pseudo-days. Then the gates: the full pipeline must first reproduce Tables 4.2 and 4.3; the model is frozen at M4 before the held-out patients are opened.
