RO1 and RO2: Information Loss and the Two-Scale ST-GNN

RO1: what is lost (Pulsewatch, Icentia11k)

  • Morphology: waveform model vs interval model on the same recordings
  • Filtering: share of ectopic beats removed by percent-deviation (Karlsson et al., 2012) and adaptive (Lipponen & Tarvainen, 2019) filters
  • Origin: all premature beats vs PAC-only count, by burden

RO2: the estimator (shown left)

  • Beat-graph per window: temporal chain plus similarity edges
  • Epoch-graph over the day: interval graph convolution and state space; on Icentia11k trained on pseudo-days (segments untimed), real order across days tested on Pulsewatch windows
  • Quality flag enters as input (Garmin flags abnormal beats and marks gaps); retention corrects only silent loss at motion (Pulsewatch, lag-corrected); pulse deficit is a bias tested in RO4a; coverage gate is a reporting rule (Equations 3.1 to 3.4, appendix)
  • Benchmarks: logistic regression, SVM, random forest, Poincaré rule, BiLSTM, GRU-D, transformer; component ablations (Appendix B)

Graph structure is tested, not assumed: an MLP slightly outperformed a GCN on visibility-graph beat features (EPMoghaddam et al., 2024).
