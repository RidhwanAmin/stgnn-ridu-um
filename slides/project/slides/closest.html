About 1 minute. This answers the panel's novelty question directly. Name the closest study first: Han et al. on the same Pulsewatch data, which classifies 30-second windows and merges PACs with PVCs. Chen et al. shows why counting is hard: PAC/PVC sensitivity 0.664, and far lower on external data. Siejko shows the logic we borrow: an imperfect beat detector can still give a valid daily burden if the count is validated at day level. Close with the three contribution types, and say plainly that the graph architecture is not itself the contribution.
