About 1.5 minutes. The pattern: ectopy predicts AF consistently, but every ectopy measure comes from a single Holter. The newest score, PAVA (Maille et al., 2026), includes a PAC term and validates well internally, which strengthens the case for ectopy, but it still needs a Holter. Clinical-only scores vary between cohorts: Palaiodimou found C2HEST best at 0.770, Mele found Brown ESUS-AF best at 0.697 and concluded none is adequate. Full Tables 1 and 2 are in the appendix.
