About 1.5 minutes. Three changes from the earlier draft. One: n is now justified, not conventional. The traffic-light method gives about 34 analysable participants for 90% power to reject the red zone if green is true; recruit about 40 to allow for dropout and wrist discomfort. Two: duration is 7 days, not 4. Three: a valid day needs coverage in both a day and a night block, because analysable time is night-weighted while PACs are day-weighted, and the daily rate is weighted by time of day rather than scaled by 24. ESVEA uses the stroke-cohort criterion of 480 or more supraventricular beats per day, from MonDAFIS, because that is the threshold shown to select stroke patients for prolonged monitoring; Binici's 30 per hour is secondary, and runs are reported separately because filtering breaks them apart. RQ3.5 is a plausibility check: at n = 34 the confidence interval on ESVEA prevalence is too wide to validate anything. Eligibility is broad, not cryptogenic-only, because a cryptogenic-only smartwatch study screened 92.5% of candidates as ineligible (Huntelaar et al., 2024).
