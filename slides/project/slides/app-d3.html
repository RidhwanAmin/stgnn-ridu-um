Direction of H1

H1 states a relationship without a direction. The preliminary data show one: at 20%, 78.5% of PAC intervals removed in ESVEA-negative patients against 60.0% in ESVEA-positive (§4.2).

Why RQ4 has none

RQ4 asks how much classification changes and how far selections agree, not whether a predicted relation holds. Fewer than five AF events are expected in the cohort, so it is descriptive: reclassification, κ, sensitivity, specificity; κ and Jaccard against scores.

Hypotheses as worded in §1.3; the If–Then form restates them and adds no claim.

Held for questions. If asked to state the hypotheses as predictions: each one names what varies and what will happen, and both sides are measured. H1: if burden differs, the share of PAC intervals surviving the filter differs; tested by mixed-effects logistic regression in patients not used in the preliminary analysis. H2: if the count comes from the flag-aware two-scale model rather than the best non-graph baseline, its error is lower; the single primary contrast. H3: if survivors wear the watch for 7 days, at least 75% give three valid days; a feasibility level against 50%, not a relation between two measured variables. RQ4 has no hypothesis because it asks how much, not whether, and too few AF events are expected to power one.
