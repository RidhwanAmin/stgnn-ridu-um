RO4b · Selection (cohort)

Wearable-based selection vs CHA₂DS₂-VASc, HAVOC, C₂HEST, AS5F and Brown ESUS-AF (ESUS subgroup only), at equal monitoring capacity: each selects as many participants as the wearable calls ESVEA-positive.

Metrics: Cohen κ; Jaccard overlap; profile of discordant patients

Substrate: each selection vs left atrial volume, P-wave duration, NT-proBNP

Descriptive: no outcome is collected, so no net reclassification improvement.

Precedent: smartwatch algorithms simulated on continuous implantable-monitor records, burden r > 0.97 (Aguilar et al., 2026). Garmin PPG short-term variability agrees only moderately with ECG after stroke, rMSSD ρc = 0.66 (Theurl et al., 2023).

About 1.5 minutes. The substitution claim is now tested where truth exists, on patients held out from RO1 calibration and RO2 training, with degradation sampled independently of the training simulation, so it is not an in-distribution test. The degradation model has four operators, each calibrated from data, including the timing jitter between PPG peaks and ECG R-peaks, the main ECG-to-PPG difference; and results are reported across severities, not one setting. Pulse deficit matters because a premature beat can fill the ventricle too little to produce a wrist pulse, so the watch sees one long interval instead of short-then-long. Zheng et al. (2008) found the shortest coupling interval with a visible pulse was 373 to 531 ms, about 55% of the sinus RR. No PAC-specific missed-pulse rate exists, so this is an assumption stated openly. RO4b is descriptive because NRI needs events and non-events (Pencina et al., 2014), and expected AF at n = 34 would be about 3 events.
